## VALHALLA: beverage branding & packaging
Source: `CERVEZA.pdf` (33 pp.), `Portfolio_reducido.pdf` pp. 20–25, attachments 6, 27–30.

1. **Name and purpose**: visual identity and packaging for a range of beers inspired by Nordic aesthetics and imagery.
2. **Context / problem**: academic exercise (Escola del Treball, module 1483 *Diseño Estructural Envase y Embalaje*, task RA5.A9.T1). Self-set goal: avoid "the typical bottle with the same style we already know".
3. **Target audience**: not documented. Implied: social / leisure drinking ("reunión y compañía… en tus momentos de ocio").
4. **Research**: Nordic bottles and boxes, fonts, illustrations, ornaments. Moodboard on p. 7 (includes third-party brands).
5. **Concept**: Valhalla = gathering and company with the people you enjoy most.
6. **Strategy**: one system, three varieties differentiated by colour.
7. **Visual identity**: Viking head and drakkar illustrations, Celtic knot medallion, Nordic border patterns, tagline "THE REAL NORDIC".
8. **Typography**: Njord Alternate (brand); Montserrat Light / Medium / SemiBold (information).
9. **Colour**: Beige #EADCBF, Night blue #1C2C3E (Beer); Black #000000 + Gold #CBAD4E (Gold); Lemon #F7EE8B + Black (Radler). Black is listed twice in the PDF palette.
10. **Graphic elements**: border friezes, runic ring, knot.
11. **Process**: silhouette in Illustrator → Esko Studio "Girar y añadir etiquetas" (front, back and cap labels) → box silhouette → Esko "Doblar archivo ArtiosCAD".
12. **Technical development**: dielines for 3 two-bottle carriers with handle and windows; labelling regulation icons; vegan certification logo.
13. **Software**: Adobe Illustrator, Esko Studio (plugin), ArtiosCAD (via Esko).
14. **Applications**: labels (2 front styles + back + cap) × 3, bottles, boxes, tap handles, ice bucket, lifestyle scenes.
15. **Prototypes**: 3D renders only. No physical prototype is documented.
16. **Final result**: render and mockup presentations (pp. 27–33, attachments).
17. **Testing**: not documented.
18. **Team**: individual (only Iván on the cover).

⚠️ *Beer* is described as "sabor suave a diferencia de las otras", while *Radler* is "mucho más suave que la principal". This is contradictory; the site avoids comparing smoothness.
⚠️ Whether Iván drew the illustrations is **not documented**: the PDF says he "searched for resources such as typefaces or illustrations". The site does not credit him with illustration.

## CIRQUERA: visual identity & communication
Source: `CIRQUERA.pdf` (43 pp.), `Portfolio_reducido.pdf` pp. 27–35, attachments 8, 12–16, 19, 32.

1. **Name / purpose**: identity for a **fictional** brand conceived as a digital platform connecting profiles, trades and disciplines of the circus world (described as a "plataforma de empleo").
2. **Problem**: bring the magic, craft and nostalgia of the circus into a clear, accessible, professional digital environment.
3. **Audience**: circus professionals and those hiring talent ("¿Buscas talento o buscas pista?"). Implied, not formally researched.
4. **Research**: not documented beyond the reasoning given in the manual.
5. **Concept**: **"Tradición Digital"**.
6. **Strategy**: the rhombus as the constructive element of the whole system (community, network of contacts).
7. **Logo**: Aquavit with a modified "e" for movement; outline variant (2 pt); grid based on X = stroke of i, r, q; minimum size 100 mm / 591 px print and 295 px digital; safety area in X.
8. **Typography**: Aquavit Extrabold / Semibold (titles); Poppins Regular / Medium / Semibold / Italic (text). Leading rules: Aquavit +3–4 pt, Poppins +2–2.5 pt.
9. **Colour**: Burgundy #37101C (PMS 4975), Red #E53131 (PMS 1788), Beige #F6ECC9 (PMS 600), each with a documented role.
10. **Graphic elements**: flat-with-grain illustration, rhombus nets, filled geometric icons; red only for active / hover.
11. **Photography direction**: cinematic, theatrical warm light; categories Talent / Environments / Detail; no front flash, no cold stock.
12. **Ad structure**: vertical (image leads, text in breathing space); horizontal (image and text on opposite sides).
13. **Software**: not documented.
14. **Applications**: business cards (×3 mockups), poster / mupi, Morris column, billboard, tote bag, Instagram post, two app screens.
15. **Prototypes**: mockups. **The app is a set of interface screens, not a functional app**, and the site says so.
16. **Result**: complete manual + application mockups.
17. **Testing**: the leading choice is justified "after several tests". Nothing else is documented.
18. **Team**: Iván Ordóñez, Oscar Garcia, Alex López, Sindri Faldmo. **Individual responsibilities not documented.**

⚠️ Manual index numbering is inconsistent (two sections numbered "2"; dividers run 01–08; page "3149" should be 39). Not reproduced on the site.

## BE STAR: cosmetic branding & packaging
Source: `CREMA.pdf` (24 pp.), `Portfolio_reducido.pdf` pp. 14–19, attachments 1, 5, 11.

1. **Name / purpose**: identity and packaging for a natural-cosmetics brand. Product: **Hydrastar**, 80 ml facial moisturiser.
2. **Context**: same academic module as Valhalla.
3. **Audience**: not documented ("todo tipo de piel" on the label copy).
4. **Research**: minimalist moisturiser brands.
5. **Concept**: the name represents people's well-being; the **star** is the main graphic element (works as imagotype and isotype).
6. **Strategy**: minimalist, natural, balanced; green for freshness, gold for premium.
7. **Identity**: star symbol, script logotype.
8. **Typography**: Sparkling Valentine (brand name only); Bricolage Grotesque (everything else).
9. **Colour**: Forest green #324425, Sage #6A8157, Star yellow #EACF4B, with documented uses.
10. **Graphic elements**: star, eco seal ("Respetuoso con el medio ambiente / Belleza saludable"), watercolour leaves on the sleeve.
11. **Process**: jar silhouette (lid metal / cream thick liquid / body glass) → Esko 3D → box with front window (2D dieline, 3D fold) → Esko Studio Toolkit simulations.
12. **Technical**: finishes: die-cut label for transparency, **Scodix Foil Gold**, coloured metallic ink, matte paper with coating and mask. Regulation icons: estimated quantity, vegan, recyclable, PAO 9 M, Green Dot, CE, read leaflet, cruelty free, barcode.
13. **Software**: Adobe Illustrator, Esko Studio, Esko Studio Toolkit, ArtiosCAD (via Esko).
14. **Applications**: jar, box, sleeve.
15. **Prototypes**: 3D renders; no physical prototype documented.
16. **Result**: renders + presentation scenes.
17. **Testing**: the 3D simulations were used to "adjust placement, check legibility and balance". This is the only testing documented.
18. **Team**: individual.

⚠️ Values: the portfolio says "Natural · Sostenible · Bienestar"; CREMA says "naturalidad, elegancia y sencillez". These are compatible, and the site uses the portfolio's three words.

## HERB LUBALIN: editorial fascicle
Source: `herb_lubalin.pdf` (3 pp.), `Portfolio_reducido.pdf` pp. 9–12, attachments 10, 21–26, 31.

- Fascicle presenting Lubalin's career and contribution to typography, in a visual language inspired by his style. Modular grid, strong hierarchy, palette reduced to red / black / white.
- Cover "I ♥ HERB LUBALIN"; spreads "Biografía + Tipografías" and "Dissenys editorials".
- **Third-party content**: Lubalin's works (No More War, Playstreet, Fact, First Harvest, Saturday Evening Post, PBS) are reference reproductions and are labelled "Obra de terceros" on the site.
- Iván's stated learning: layout, typographic composition, editorial design; balance of function and expression.
